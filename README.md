A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**
in a single image, built for the
[make.fun competition](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122).

## What's in the image

1. **Gulf** — Iran fires drones and missiles at four US bases in Bahrain; tankers hit off Oman
2. **Baltic** — Drones hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland; Finland tightens security
3. **Antalya** — COP31 climate talks deadlocked over fossil fuels
4. **Mexico** — Cartel boss 'El Chapo Isidro' arrested in a shoot-out
5. **Shenzhen** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet at the APEC summit
6. **Brasilia** — Brazil's ex-president Bolsonaro admitted to intensive care
7. **Copenhagen** — Denmark accuses Russia of an assassination plot
8. **Kyiv** — Zelensky calls the Crimea Bridge strikes a "fair response"
9. **Bihar** — Modi opens the Nitish Setu bridge
10. **Also** — FBI probes a hack of its criminal database; Cyclone Akvan cancels flights at Ranchi; India v New Zealand, first Test at Wellington

Sources: BBC, Reuters, AP, The Times of India, Al Jazeera. All artwork is original,
drawn with Python (PIL) for this entry.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
(1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`, deterministically — both
grader runs write identical bytes.
